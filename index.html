About me

I am a PhD student at LIRIS, a research laboratory of the Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS), and Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 in Lyon, France. I am advised by Prof. Angela Bonifati.

My doctoral research is supported by the ERC Advanced Grant GO-Y, Unifying Graph Databases and Causal Models. My research interests span graph data management, causal graph databases, and privacy-preserving graph computing.

Research interests

Graph data management
Graph databases, graph query processing, and efficient methods for large and knowledge graphs.
Causal graph databases
Data management foundations that connect graph database systems with causal models.
Privacy-preserving graph computing
Secure and scalable graph analytics, including multi-party computation over sensitive graph data.
