About me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 and a member of LIRIS, a research laboratory affiliated with the Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS), in Lyon, France. I am advised by Prof. Angela Bonifati.

My PhD is supported by the ERC Advanced Grant project GO-Y (Unifying Graph Databases and Causal Models). My current research focuses on graph data management and causality-driven graph databases.

Before starting my PhD, I earned an MPhil in Computer Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Chenhao Ma. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2024, where I worked with Prof. Jing Wang.
