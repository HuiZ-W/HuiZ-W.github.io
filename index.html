About me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 and a member of LIRIS, a research laboratory affiliated with the Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS), in Lyon, France. I am advised by Prof. Angela Bonifati.

My PhD is supported by the ERC Advanced Grant project GO-Y (Unifying Graph Databases and Causal Models). My current research focuses on graph data management and causal graph databases.
